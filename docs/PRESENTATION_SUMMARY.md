# SchoolLib: Reimagining the School Library
## Presentation Summary

---

## 📚 What is SchoolLib?
SchoolLib is a modern, digital library management system designed specifically for schools. It replaces old-fashioned paper logs and outdated software with a clean, easy-to-use website. 

The goal of SchoolLib is simple: make it easier for **Students** to find and borrow books, and make it easier for **Librarians (Admin & Staff)** to track and manage the library's inventory.

---

## 👥 Who Uses SchoolLib?

The system is built for three different types of users, each with their own unique dashboard:

### 1. The Student
* **What they do:** Browse the library catalog from their phone or laptop.
* **Key Benefits:**
  * **Discover Books:** Easily search for books, use color-coded category tags, and see what books are currently available.
  * **Reserve Online:** No need to wait in line. Students can click a button to request a book and select the exact date they plan to return it.
  * **Stay on Track:** The dashboard shows exactly how many books they are allowed to borrow (Borrowing Limits) and displays bright yellow or red alerts when a book is due soon or overdue.
  * **Share Opinions:** After returning a book, students can leave a star rating and a written review for their classmates to see.
  * **Personalize:** Students can switch the website's language (English, Filipino, Cebuano) and choose dark mode or custom colors.

### 2. The Library Staff
* **What they do:** Run the day-to-day operations of the library.
* **Key Benefits:**
  * **Manage Inventory:** Easily add new books, upload cover images, and organize them into physical shelves.
  * **Process Requests:** See a list of all students waiting to borrow books. Staff can approve the request, or change the return date if needed and leave a note for the student.
  * **Quick Borrow & Return:** A fast, simple tool to issue a book to a student in person, or check a book back into the system so someone else can read it.

### 3. The Administrator
* **What they do:** Oversee the entire system and manage the rules.
* **Key Benefits:**
  * **Total Control:** Admins can view analytics charts showing library usage and easily export transaction logs to spreadsheets.
  * **Library Rules:** They control the global "Borrowing Limit" (e.g., setting the rule that no student can borrow more than 3 books at once).
  * **Manage the Team:** Only admins can create new staff accounts or permanently delete old records from the archive.
  * **Promote Books:** Admins can spotlight special books by marking them as "Featured" or crowning one as the "Book of the Month" to display prominently on student dashboards.

---

## ⭐ The Standout Features

Here is what makes SchoolLib special compared to traditional systems:

* **🤖 Libby, The AI Assistant:** A built-in smart chatbot. Instead of asking a busy librarian, a student can ask Libby, *"Do you have any books about space?"* Libby knows the exact books the library owns, if they are currently available, and exactly which shelf to find them on.
* **🏷️ Smart Tagging System:** Books aren't just limited to one genre. They can have multiple custom tags like "Award Winner", "Summer Reading", or "Staff Pick," making it incredibly easy for students to filter and find their next read.
* **🔔 Automated Alerts & Notifications:** The system does the remembering for you. Students receive pop-up notifications when their book request is approved, and warning banners automatically appear on their dashboard days before a book is due.
* **🌐 Multilingual & Accessible:** Built for diverse student bodies, the entire interface instantly translates to local languages like Filipino and Cebuano with a single click.
* **🗑️ Safe Deletion (Archive):** If a staff member accidentally deletes a book or a transaction, it isn't gone forever. It goes to a digital "Archive" where an admin can safely restore it.

---

## 🎯 Conclusion
SchoolLib transforms the school library from a quiet room of physical books into an interactive, 24/7 digital hub. It gives students the modern web experience they expect, while giving librarians the powerful organizational tools they need.
